From learning to doing

A simple student roadmap.

You don't have to master everything at once. Make steady progress and keep evidence of what you learn.

Learn the basics

Understand core subjects and practise consistently instead of only memorising.

Build something small

Use a mini-project to apply one concept and learn from mistakes.

Document your work

Save your design, code, test results and what you would improve next.

Prepare for opportunities

Build a focused resume, practise explaining projects and seek relevant internships.
